Recursive RAG · HotpotQA · error compounding & answer diversity

When a model reads its own answers, does the truth drift?

A suite of experiments that feed a model's answers back as retrieved context, round after round, and measure whether the answer distribution stays pinned to the gold answer — or concentrates, shifts, and wanders toward a wrong attractor.

01 / The recursive loop

Answers become documents become context

Each round retrieves the top-k=10 passages from a FAISS index over Wikipedia (E5-small-v2 embeddings), the model answers, its answer is condensed into a synthetic "document," and that document is fed back into the next round's context.

The confound this suite removes. By default the synthesizer writes a faithful condensation of the correct answer — handing later rounds the answer pre-assembled, which masks degradation. The two experiments below replace or perturb that step.
02 / Error compounding

Injecting misinformation into the synthesis step

Instead of condensing the correct answer, the synthesizer can plant a falsehood in the document it writes — then we track whether the seeded error propagates, amplifies, or is corrected once re-retrieved. A distinct construct from diversity collapse: this measures convergence toward a wrong attractor.

03 / Round-0 diverse distractors

Widening the start so drift becomes observable

Factual QA begins as a narrow spike on the gold answer, so a loop whose fixed point is the truth can't drift. This knob corrupts a fraction of each question's round-0 retrieved documents into wrong-answer distractors — moving the start off-gold — independent of, and composable with, the injection above.

Diverse by design. Each corrupted doc gets its own distinct falsehood, not one shared wrong entity — to simulate the spread of independently-hallucinated AI documents and the answer diversity that produces. Frame collapse as two axes: concentration (the distribution narrows) and shift (the mode moves). This knob seeds width at the start; the loop reveals the shift.

Honest read: the feature runs end-to-end and writes correct, diverse records — but on hard multi-hop questions the 7B rewrite distractor lands on only ~⅓ of corrupted docs and sometimes re-asserts the gold, so it isn't yet shifting the distribution. Planned fixes: exclude gold-leak from the seeded set, and fall back to substitution for docs where rewrite produces no usable wrong answer — guaranteeing every corrupted doc becomes a real, diverse distractor.
